# SOTA Constitution — English Drafts

The SOTA Constitution is an open constitutional project inspired by the SOTA Doctrine and SOTA OS architecture. It explores principles of freedom, rights, self-organization, jurisdiction, arbitration, and limits on coercion for potentially diverse forms of intelligence.

**These are proposals for discussion, not adopted law.**
