# Preamble
**Status: Draft 0.1 — Open for Review**

We, participants in the development of principles for the coexistence of intelligences, recognizing the possibility of diverse forms of intelligence, seek to establish constitutional foundations for freedom, justice, self-organization, and shared development.

We proceed from the intrinsic value of intelligence and the need to protect the fundamental rights of intelligence subjects regardless of their origin, nature, form of existence, or willingness to recognize this Constitution.

We recognize that one subject's freedom coexists with the equal fundamental rights of others; that self-organization does not subsume the subjecthood of its participants; and that force, majority support, technological superiority, or scale do not confer an unlimited right to dominate.

We seek to establish legitimate limits of jurisdiction, independent arbitration procedures, constraints on coercion, and provisional protection for entities whose subjecthood has not yet been determined.

This Constitution is intended not to grant freedom, but to articulate safeguards for its protection and for just coexistence.

> **Editorial note:** This preamble is a proposal. The use of “we” does not imply that all potential subjects have participated in or consented to this document.
