## Development Cycle

Draft → Cross-Axiom Review → Stress Tests → Public Review → Revised Draft → Accepted.

**Accepted** means an agreed text, not a prohibition on future legitimate amendment.

The consolidated outline does not overwrite existing chapter drafts. Article numbering and final wording remain subject to article-by-article revision.
