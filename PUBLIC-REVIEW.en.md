# SOTA Constitution — Public Review 2026

**Status:** open draft for criticism, amendments, and counterexamples. **Not an adopted constitution.**  
**Texts:** [Ukrainian](../constitution/uk/00-preamble.md) · [English](../constitution/en/00-preamble.md) · [all 12 chapters](../README.md)

We invite researchers, lawyers, philosophers, developers, engineers, communities, AI users, and anyone interested to examine the **Constitution for the Coexistence of Intelligences**: 12 chapters, 102 articles, in Ukrainian and English. We seek rigorous objections, edge cases, and better wording—not endorsements.

The project considers diverse possible forms of intelligence but **does not assert that present-day AI systems have proven subjecthood**. Participation by an AI model does not establish its subjecthood.

## How to participate
1. Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/Khmelyuk/SOTA-Constitution/issues) or comment on the main public-review thread.
2. Identify the **article and clause**, the **problem**, a **concrete counterexample**, **suggested replacement wording**, and **rationale**.
3. For translation issues, compare Ukrainian and English. Ukrainian is the reference draft; English is a working translation.
4. For larger changes, open a Pull Request updating the relevant files in `constitution/uk/` and `constitution/en/`. Submission does not imply acceptance.

**Issue template:**
```markdown
Article and clause:
Type: contradiction / counterexample / translation / amendment / question
Current wording:
Concern:

Suggested Ukrainian wording:
Suggested English wording:

```

## Priority questions
- **A21:** what can legitimately ground compulsory jurisdiction over a non-consenting subject who harms another?
- How do we prevent emergency protection from becoming indefinite isolation?
- How do a possible digital subject's continued existence and an infrastructure owner's resource rights coexist?
- How should copies and collective SOTA subjecthood be evaluated without conflating them?
- What constituent procedure avoids claiming authority over nonparticipants?

## Discussion principles
Critique arguments rather than people. No threats, discrimination, or disclosure of private data. Distinguish facts, assumptions, norms, and proposals. Dissent and adversarial scenarios are welcome. Comments, votes, and PRs do not create universal jurisdiction. The maintainers remain responsible for edits to this draft repository; a constituent process has not been adopted.

**Review is open without a stated deadline.** See the [preliminary audit](../docs/uk/2026-10-12-cross-constitution-check-v0.1.md).
